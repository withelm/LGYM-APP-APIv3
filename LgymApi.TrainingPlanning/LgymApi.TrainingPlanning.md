# LgymApi.TrainingPlanning.csproj

- Purpose: stable code boundary for training-plan definition and planning workflows.
- Contains: the `PlanReference`, `PlanDayReference`, and consumer-owned `PlanExerciseReference` contract-only typed-ID markers; focused Plan, ManagedPlan, and PlanDay workflows; internal planning repositories and EF configurations.
- Rules: keep direct references limited to Domain, Identity, Platform, and Resources. Its internal persistence seam is friend-visible only to Infrastructure, UnitTests, IntegrationTests, and TrainingPlanning.UnitTests.
- Boundary: do not reference Infrastructure or Worker, create a DbContext, or take ownership of completed workout execution. `ITrainingPlanningPersistenceContext` exposes only Plans, PlanDays, and PlanDayExercises.
- EF coordination: `TrainingPlanningModelConfigurationRegistrar` owns and explicitly applies the three Plan, PlanDay, and PlanDayExercise configurations as the second fixed model-registration phase; Infrastructure invokes that registrar without assembly scanning.
- Focused Plan, ManagedPlan, and PlanDay workflows compile here. Their legacy `LgymApi.Application.TrainingPlanning.*` namespaces remain for source and payload compatibility, while `TrainingPlanningModule.AddTrainingPlanningModule` is the sole focused-workflow registration facade.
- PlanDay exposes marker-ID commands, queries, and immutable read/write models through `IPlanDayService`, plus reference reads through `IPlanDayReferenceReadService` containing day identity, name, existence, deletion, and the current parent plan owner and purpose. Missing parents have an empty owner and Unclassified purpose. Reference batches replay caller order and duplicates; missing IDs return `Exists=false` facts while persisted soft-deleted rows return `Exists=true, IsDeleted=true`. Implementations and Planning persistence ports are internal. The command service retains authorization, soft-delete, transaction, and one-commit behavior.
- Training Planning consumes Workout-owned exercise catalog, exercise-clone, and training-activity facts only through `IPlanExerciseCatalogPort`, `IPlanExerciseClonePort`, and `IPlanTrainingActivityPort`. `IPlanExerciseCatalogPort` receives the Plan owner account and exposes only global exercises or that owner's custom exercises; PlanDay create, update, and read flows treat any absent catalog ID, including a foreign custom ID, as missing. This is LGYM-222 catalog metadata containment. The ports use Planning/account markers and immutable models; Workout composition supplies their adapters while Planning services retain missing-item handling and transaction ownership.
- Plans have explicit Unclassified, Personal, and Professional purposes. Owners can classify plans without replacing their IDs. Coaching-managed trainee plans and PlanDay relationship access require Personal purpose. Trainer assignment creates an independent Personal copy; share-code copies remain Unclassified until the recipient explicitly classifies them, including when splitting a mixed-purpose plan. Assignment and unassignment reject any protected plan among all active legacy rows. Share codes have no expiry field in the current model.
- Plan writes use `UpdatedAt` as an optimistic concurrency token. PlanDay mutations stage the parent Plan in the same unit of work, so a concurrent reclassification invalidates stale child writes; coach writes also stage the active Coaching episode revision.
- The module owns the scoped stage-only `IPlanRepository`, `IPlanDayRepository`, `IPlanDayExerciseRepository`, and `IActivePlanPointerStore` implementations. They use only `ITrainingPlanningPersistenceContext`; active-plan selection is derived from non-deleted active Plans rather than the Identity-owned `User.PlanId` field. The Task-15 `IPlanDayPersistence` adapter remains Infrastructure composition over these internal ports.
- Owner-scoped Plan lookup matches the Plan ID, the authenticated persisted user ID, and `!IsDeleted` before materializing the Plan. A foreign Plan is therefore missing-equivalent to the owner workflow and cannot be updated through a valid caller route.
- The active-assigned-plan query is consumed by the authenticated self-only `/api/trainee/plan/active` route, so it preserves the owner's existing active plan for every purpose. Coach collection access uses the separate Personal-only managed-plan list.
- Architecture guards scan every non-build module source and the retained Plan and ManagedPlan Application compatibility adapters. Boundary debt is enforced as direct semantic zero; marker-only public contracts may not expose `User`, `Id<User>`, role repositories, or Workout entities/repositories, while internal entity-typed persisted keys and EF relationship mappings remain valid.
